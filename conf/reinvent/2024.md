- NET209 | Designing your AWS network for growth and flexibility

VPC peering and building up to a multi-Region network using `AWS Transit Gateway` and `AWS Cloud WAN`

This builders’ session explores two of those managed services,
`AWS Cloud WAN` and `AWS Network Firewall`, through the build-out of a solution that allows you to architect and implement outbound traffic controls.
